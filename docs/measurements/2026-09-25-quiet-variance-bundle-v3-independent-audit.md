# Independent audit of the v3 calibration candidate

**Decision: not qualified for the planned 16 model runs.** An independent read-only audit of the pinned private harness and five public qualification controls found three campaign-level gaps. The [candidate protocol](data/2026-09-25-quiet-variance-calibration-bundle-v3-candidate.json) remains pending, its `independent_review` evidence slot remains `null`, and `model_run_authorized` remains `false`. This audit is a finding record, not a positive qualification artifact.

The reviewer checked the 14 pinned source files, both CLI binary hashes and version, five evidence pins and four prospective control plans, the reproduced 16-slot balanced schedule, source and runtime fingerprints, eight raw CLI traces, 16 read-only SQLite usage ledgers, and seven evaluator reports and their linked raw outputs. The five controls support their stated narrow observations. Full CLI/provider controls were not rerun during this review, and historic push timing and source ancestry were not independently reconstructed. The review began at `b9ec12b676948d79db8b58da27c5c6e42e76048f`; the subsequent `52c1daadf339555ccae973e80d666425c8fc8b43` commit changed only an unrelated research note.

## Qualification blockers

1. The [campaign runner](../measurements/fixtures/2026-09-25-quiet-variance-calibration-bundle-v3-runner.py) seals only a captured checkpoint with a quality report. The scored cleanup-negative control has neither, so `evidence_hashes()` raises before writing a terminal `finish` event. In a read-only reproduction with mocked launch and journal write, the journal contained only `start`: the next slot did not launch, but no terminal reason or `unstarted_ids` report was formed. The run's existing usage and `final.json` were not lost. A revision must seal available failure evidence and report all unstarted slots without retrying the stopped slot.
2. The [integrated gate](../measurements/fixtures/2026-09-25-quiet-variance-bundle-v3-integrated-gate.py) pins the base Python tree and loaded module origins; the candidate campaign runner pins its virtual environment but does not enforce that base-runtime contract before and after each slot. The base runtime currently matches the control pin, but future drift would not be blocked by the campaign runner. The OS and external toolchain also remain outside the frozen runtime.
3. The prospective [analysis plan](../research/2026-09-25-quiet-variance-calibration-plan.md) specifies tokens per accepted repair, paired differences and ratios, dispersion, order effects, and handling of failed or unstarted slots. No executable final 16-run reducer is pinned in the candidate. A revision must freeze that reducer and exercise failed repairs, zero accepted repairs, accounted timeouts, and incomplete campaigns before model calls.

The positive control's timeout was an early CLI cancellation with time left in the shared deadline; it did not exercise expiration of the campaign deadline. Extra Click regression groups run in the normal-control gate but not in the ordinary evaluator. The packaging upstream inventory is a 291-test metadata subset. Synthetic provider usage tests accounting logic, not authentic billing; injected cleanup uncertainty tests the response to that receipt, not detection of every escaped process.

The required next step is a new prospective protocol revision and independent requalification of the changed campaign paths. Even a completed 16-run calibration would cover two previously exposed tasks, with hooks and plugins disabled. It would not establish savings or quality preservation for installed SoL Codex.
